Dough method & baking notes
  1. Mix all flour and water; cover and rest 20 minutes.
  2. Add salt, sugar and yeast. Knead until incorporated; rest another 20 minutes (optional).
  3. Knead 5–7 minutes and form a ball.
  4. Cover and rise until doubled or tripled, about 1–3 hours.
  5. Divide, form tight dough balls, and refrigerate 2–3 days. For same-day dough, rest at room temperature another 2–3 hours.
  6. Preheat oven and baking steel to 550°F for about 90 minutes.
  7. Top sauce, Pecorino and mozzarella. Bake 4–5 minutes.
